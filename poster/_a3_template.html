Statistical methods

  • Central tendency & dispersion: mean, median, mode, variance, SD, IQR.
  • Position: quartiles Q1–Q3; percentiles P5, P90, P95, P99.
  • Shape: Fisher skewness, excess kurtosis. Outliers: Tukey 1.5 × IQR fence.
  • Association: Spearman ρ — preferred to Pearson because every variable is heavily right-skewed and the relations are monotonic, not linear.
  • Group comparison: Kruskal–Wallis H (non-parametric one-way ANOVA).
  • Normality: Shapiro–Wilk W on a random sub-sample (n = 4,000).
  • Logarithmic axes where ranges span several orders of magnitude.

Cleaning decisions

  • De-duplicated on bug ID, keeping the first occurrence.
  • Negative durations (resolved before created) set to missing.
  • Resolutions under 15 min excluded from Figs 2–3 as automated closures.
  • Legacy severity words excluded (< 0.6% of records; the repository had migrated to the S1–S4 scale before this window).
